Privacy Policy

Redline Tokyo: Street Racing – last updated September 29, 2026

This policy explains what data the game Redline Tokyo ("the game") by Ramzi Mammadli ("we", "the developer") collects, why, and how you can delete it.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or any payment information.
  • We store only what is needed to run the game: a player ID, your driver name, game progress and leaderboard results.
  • You can delete your account and all its cloud data in the game at any time.

Services we use

The game uses Unity Gaming Services (provided by Unity Technologies) to run its online features: Authentication, Cloud Save, Leaderboards, Lobby and Relay. Unity processes the data below on our behalf only to provide these features. See Unity's privacy policy at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Data we collect

  • Player ID – when you first start the game an anonymous player ID is created by Unity Authentication. It is used to save your progress and identify your entries on leaderboards.
  • Optional account – if you choose to create an account, you pick a username and password. These are stored and verified by Unity Authentication; the developer never sees your password.
  • Driver name – the display name you choose, shown on leaderboards and to other players in online races.
  • Gameplay content – race times and ghost replays submitted to leaderboards, and your game progress (cars, upgrades, colours, currency, owned items) stored in Cloud Save.
  • Purchase history – purchases are processed entirely by Apple (In-App Purchase) or Google Play (Play Billing). We never receive your payment details. The game only records which items you own in your cloud save so they can be restored.
  • Online sessions – when you race online, Lobby and Relay temporarily process connection data (such as your player ID and IP address) to connect you with other players. This data is used only for the match.

How we use data

Data is used only to provide the game's functionality: saving and restoring progress, leaderboards, online multiplayer and delivering purchases. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with data brokers. It is not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete it at any time in the game: Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and its cloud data (progress, driver name and leaderboard entries). You can also request deletion through the support page.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the game data described above, and it contains no ads or tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
