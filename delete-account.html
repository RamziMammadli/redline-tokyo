Delete your account and data – Redline Tokyo: Street Racing, by Ramzi Mammadli / Vivim Technologies

Option 1 – in the game (instant)

  1. Open Redline Tokyo on your phone, tablet or Mac.
  2. Tap Profile at the top of the main menu.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and its cloud data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 – without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, send us a deletion request:

Open a deletion request

Include your driver name and, if you created one, your account username. Do not post passwords or payment details. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm in the request.

What is deleted

  • Your account (username) and anonymous player ID
  • Cloud save: progress, cars, money and gold
  • Your driver name, leaderboard entries and ghost replays
  • Your friends list

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept by us after deletion. Records of in-app purchases are kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own policies; we never receive your payment details.
  • Progress stored only on your device stays there until you uninstall the game.
